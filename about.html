Master Craftsmanship

Our 4 Core Pillars of Excellence

Every event space we transform is guided by unyielding safety standards, artistic lighting architecture, and flawless drapery execution.

01

Photometric Lighting Architecture

We meticulously map color temperatures (2700K-3200K) and beam angles so every table, floral arrangement, and backdrop shines flawlessly in photography.

02

Fire-Retardant Fabric Tailoring

We exclusively source NFPA 701 certified IFR sheer chiffon, velvet, and satin drapes with custom hardware to create sweeping ceiling canopies safely.

03

Certified Structural Rigging

Our master riggers hold structural load certifications for overhead crystal chandelier hanging, trussing, and fail-safe power distribution.

04

White-Glove Live Monitoring

Dedicated lighting technicians remain on standby to smoothly transition ambient scenes from romantic dinner candlelight to energetic party dancing.

Our Journey

Illuminating Milestones

From boutique uplighting rental to a full-service custom event lighting and draping company.

2014

Boutique Lighting Studio

Founded with 20 wireless LED uplights and a vision to replace harsh fluorescent venue lights with warm ambient glow.

2017

Ceiling Draping Division

Added custom fabric drapery hardware and flame-resistant sheer chiffon canopies to transform raw spaces into romantic ballrooms.

2021

Luxury Chandelier & Rigging

Acquired a high-end inventory of crystal and vintage brass chandeliers along with certified overhead rigging trusses.

2026

Industry Benchmark

Over 1,200 weddings and luxury corporate galas transformed with custom ambient lighting and ceiling draping perfection.
